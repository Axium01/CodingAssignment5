Classic Waffles

This recipe comes from "Classic Waffles" by Megan, found on https://www.allrecipes.com/recipe/20513/classic-waffles/

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar
  • 4 teaspoons baking powder
  • 1 teaspoon salt
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 ½ cups warm milk
  • ⅓ cup butter, melted
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

Equipment

  • Waffle iron
  • 2 large bowls
  • Whisk
  • Measuring cup(s)
  • Teaspoon and tablespoon

Directions

  1. Mix flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt together in a large bowl until well blended; set aside. Preheat a waffle iron to desired temperature.
  2. Beat eggs in a separate bowl; stir in milk, melted butter, and vanilla until well combined.
  3. Pour milk mixture into flour mixture; beat until blended.
  4. Ladle batter into the preheated waffle iron.
  5. Cook waffles until golden and crisp.
  6. Serve immediately and enjoy!
